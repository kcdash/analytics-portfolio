Retention simulator

Choose how the offer audience is picked and how many customers to contact. Every result comes from measured treated-versus-holdout differences in nine campaigns the models never saw.

About 79,000 customers are eligible per campaign. 30% is close to today's 25,000 contacts. 100% is the offer cost assumed in the deck (4% to 20% of the basket). Response is held fixed, so use this to test the cost assumption, not to design offers. 100% is the recorded cost: $0.02 email, $0.06 SMS, $0.95 direct mail.
